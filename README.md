# Neon Coast 🌴

An original open-world browser game seen from above: a tropical coastal city with neon lights, driving, police and missions.
It is inspired by open-world crime-and-driving games, but the city, names and characters are original.

## How to open
Double-click `index.html`. Nothing to install.

## What is in the game
- A whole city: a downtown with towers, a beach strip, a market, a suburb, a port, a park and swamps
- Several kinds of cars, road traffic and pedestrians
- Police with up to 5 stars, and spray shops that clear them
- 6 story missions and 15 hidden golden flamingo statues
- A day and night cycle with street lamps, headlights and neon signs
- Radio, engine and sirens, all generated in code
- Automatic saving in the browser (money, missions, flamingos)

## Controls
- **On foot:** arrow keys or WASD, Shift to run, E to get into a car
- **In a car:** ↑ gas, ↓ brake and reverse, ← → steer, Space handbrake, E exit, H horn, R radio
- **General:** P or Esc for pause and the map, M to mute
- **Phone:** drag a finger like a joystick, the 🚗 button to get in and out, and a handbrake button

## Files
- `index.html`, `style.css` – structure and styling
- `js/map.js` – city generation
- `js/world.js` – cars, physics, pedestrians, traffic and police
- `js/missions.js` – the missions
- `js/render.js` – drawing
- `js/audio.js` – sounds
- `js/main.js` – input, interface and the game loop
- `hebrew-backup/` – a copy of the earlier Hebrew version
