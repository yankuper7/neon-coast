How to Play

On foot

WASD or arrows – walk

Shift – run

E – get into the nearest car

Driving

↑ gas · ↓ brake / reverse · ←→ steer

Space handbrake · H horn · R music

E get out · T taxi fares / retry a mission

Phone and tablet

Drag anywhere on the screen like a joystick. In a car, the car drives where you point.

🚗 gets in and out · DRIFT is the handbrake · 📣 is the horn.

Prefer pedals? Switch to Pedals in Settings.

Gamepad

Left stick – move and steer · RT gas · LT brake

A in / out · B handbrake · X horn · Y action · Start pause

  • Missions: coloured circles with a letter are your contacts. Walk in, or stop your car on one, to hear the job. Several can be open at once.
  • Finding the way: the arrow and the coloured line on the radar follow the roads to your target.
  • Clocks: every second left on the clock becomes a speed bonus. Failed? Press T (or tap ↻) to try again right away.
  • Police ★: stealing cars, hitting people or ramming patrol cars earns stars. Get out of their sight and stay hidden until the countdown ends, or drive into a spray shop 🎨 (green on the map, from $100).
  • Higher stars: officers get out and chase you on foot. At four stars they ram you, block roads and send a helicopter.
  • Traffic lights: the city stops on red. You do not have to – but a patrol car might notice a crash.
  • Weather: wet roads are slippery. Brake earlier in the rain.
  • Side jobs: press T in a taxi for endless fares, and look for 🏁 time trials while driving.
  • 🦩 Golden flamingos: 15 are hidden around the city, worth $150 each.
  • Music: every district has its own tune. R switches between City Sound, four car radio stations and silence.
  • Pause and map: P or Esc · mute with M.
