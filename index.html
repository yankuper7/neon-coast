Neon Coast

Open city. Fast cars. Pink sunsets.

How to play

  • On foot: arrow keys or WASD · Shift to run · E to get into a nearby car.
  • In a car: ↑ gas · ↓ brake and reverse · ← → steer · Space handbrake · E exit · H horn · R radio.
  • On a phone: drag a finger like a joystick – the car drives where you point. The 🚗 button gets in and out, and the handbrake button is for sharp turns.
  • Missions: the yellow circle with a letter on the map is your next mission. Stand on it to start.
  • Police ★: stealing a car, hitting pedestrians or ramming a police car earns stars. Get out of the cops' sight, or drive into a spray shop 🎨 (green square on the radar, $100).
  • Your car: crashes damage it. When it pours black smoke, get out before it explodes. The spray shop also repairs it.
  • 🦩 Golden flamingos: 15 statues are hidden around the city. Each one is worth $150.
  • Pause and map: P or Esc.
